GrocGo Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026

GrocGo is a grocery list and recipe app. This policy explains what the app collects, why, who receives it and the choices you have. Questions: support@grocgo.app.

In short

  • GrocGo shows no ads, does not track you across other apps or websites, and does not sell your data.
  • Your exact location stays on your phone.
  • AI features send only what a request needs to OpenAI, and only after you agree.
  • You can delete your account and its cloud data at any time in Settings.

What GrocGo collects

Account. Your email address and the name you enter, or the name and email Apple or Google provides if you sign in with them. Passwords are stored in a form GrocGo cannot read.

Your lists and recipes. Shopping lists, items, saved recipes and favorite stores are kept on your phone. With GrocGo Plus they are also saved to your account in the cloud, so they sync between your devices and survive a new phone.

Location. While you allow location access, your phone uses it to notice which of your saved stores you are in and to find stores near you through Apple Maps. Your exact location is not sent to GrocGo’s servers. For AI meal plans and cost estimates, the app sends your city, state or region and country, never your exact location, so prices fit your area.

AI requests. AI features are optional and the app asks before the first request. Depending on the feature, the request contains your meal preferences and saved recipe names (meal plans), a recipe’s name and ingredients (cost estimates), the content of a recipe page you paste a link to, or the caption, title and subtitles of a video post. For a cooking video you choose from Photos, your phone turns the speech into text and only that text is sent; the video itself never leaves your phone.

Usage records. GrocGo keeps counts tied to your account, such as how often the app was opened and how many AI requests you made with their size and cost, to apply your plan’s limits and prevent abuse. These records do not contain your prompts, transcripts or generated recipes.

Purchases. Apple handles payment for GrocGo Plus. GrocGo receives Apple’s signed record of the purchase (the product, dates and transaction identifiers) to give your account access. GrocGo never receives your card or payment details.

Diagnostics. The app keeps a technical log on your phone for 24 hours. It leaves your phone only if you choose to report an issue from Settings, which attaches it to an email you send.

Apple Watch. If you use the watch app, your list is sent to your paired Apple Watch.

How GrocGo uses it

To run the app’s features, sync your data between your devices, create the AI results you ask for, apply your plan’s limits, answer support requests, keep the service secure, and meet legal obligations. GrocGo does not use your data for advertising.

Who receives data

GrocGo shares data only with the services that run the app:

  • Supabase hosts GrocGo’s database and sign-in service, in the United States.
  • OpenAI receives the content of the AI requests described above and returns the result.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple, in-app purchases, Apple Maps search and app distribution.
  • Google provides Sign in with Google if you choose it. When you import a YouTube video link, GrocGo uses YouTube API Services to read the video’s details; the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy apply to that use. YouTube Terms of Service · Google Privacy Policy
  • Expo delivers app updates; your phone contacts its servers to check for one.

GrocGo may also disclose information when the law requires it. GrocGo does not sell personal data and does not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

How long it is kept

Account data and cloud-saved lists and recipes are kept until you delete your account. Detailed AI request records are kept for about two months, then reduced to totals. The diagnostic log on your phone is kept for 24 hours. After an account is deleted, records of purchases and AI costs may be kept without a link to you, for accounting and fraud prevention.

Your choices

  • Location: turn it off for GrocGo in your iPhone’s Settings. You can still choose a store yourself.
  • AI features: decline the prompt. The rest of the app works without them.
  • Delete your account: Settings > Delete account permanently removes your account and everything saved to it in the cloud. Data stored only on your phone is removed when you delete the app. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription: cancel GrocGo Plus in your Apple account’s Subscriptions.
  • Access or correction: email support@grocgo.app to ask for a copy of your data or to have it corrected or deleted. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA; GrocGo honors those requests.

Children

GrocGo is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided it, email support@grocgo.app and it will be deleted.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and access to account data is limited to the account’s owner and the service itself. No system is perfectly secure, so GrocGo cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page and in the app with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be announced in the app.
