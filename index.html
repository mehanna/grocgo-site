Get GrocGo

Shop it. Cook it. In one app.

  1. Just type. It’s sorted. GrocGo finds every item’s aisle by itself and tags it with the store you pick.

  2. Walk in. Your list is ready. Walk into a store you saved and GrocGo switches your list to it by itself: only what to buy there, with nothing to tap.

  3. Tune in. Your store. Or slide the tuner to any store yourself and see only what to buy there. The rest folds away.

  4. Custom meals for your budget. Your personal AI Chef plans 10 recipes for your budget, servings and diet, each with its estimated cost.

  5. Recipes made your way. Pick your main ingredient and your personal AI Chef writes up to 10 recipes, sized for your servings.

  6. From social media to your recipes. Watching a cooking video on TikTok, Instagram or YouTube? Tap Share and pick GrocGo. It opens by itself and your AI Chef turns the video into a recipe.

  7. Any recipe link, imported. Paste a link from a recipe website or a video, or pick a cooking video from your Photos, and your AI Chef writes it up as a recipe.

  8. Your cookbook. Every recipe you save in one place, with its photo, ingredients and steps.

  9. Know the cost. And the calories. See what a recipe costs, plus its calories, protein, carbs and fat.

  10. From recipe to shopping list. Tick the ingredients you need and they land on your list, sorted by aisle.

  11. Cook it. Step by step. Big, clear steps with timers built in, and GrocGo can read each one aloud.

iPhone Duo

Closed or open. It fits.

GrocGo is ready for iPhone Duo: the app you know when it’s shut, two panes when it’s open.

  1. Closed or open. It fits. Shut, it’s the GrocGo you know. Open, your aisles spread across the screen, and the item you tap opens beside your list.

  2. Your AI Chef, wider. Open, AI Chef’s tools spread out. Tap one and it opens beside the cards.

  3. Cookbook and recipe. Open, your cookbook fills the screen. Tap a recipe and it opens beside it.

  4. Half-open for cooking. Cooking mode splits at the fold: the step above, what’s next below.
